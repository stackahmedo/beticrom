# Project Guidelines & Design System

## General Engineering Principles
- Use responsive and well-structured flexbox and grid layouts by default. Absolute positioning should only be reserved for overlays and badges.
- Keep components modular and reusable, segregating domain entities into clear directories.
- Maintain trilingual string support (`JA`, `EN`, `BN`) across all customer-facing surfaces.
- Ensure strict adherence to Japanese e-commerce standards, specifically around temperature-controlled logistics, clear currency formatting (¥), and domestic payment gateways.

## Design System Guidelines
- **Primary Brand Color:** Deep Forest Green (`#11683A`) symbolizing halal trust, natural quality, and freshness.
- **Accent Color:** Warm Gold (`#F59E0B`) for certification highlights, badges, and seasonal alerts.
- **Urgency/Promo Color:** Deep Red (`#D92D20`) for limited deals, flash sales, and price reductions.
- **Background:** Warm Off-White (`#FAF8F3`) providing warmth and reading comfort.
- **Typography:**
  - Japanese display & body: `Noto Sans JP`
  - Latin numbers & headings: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Filled with brand green (`#11683A`), white text, bold font weight.
  - *Secondary:* Outlined with border, clean hover background.
  - *Promo/Action:* Red filled button for instant deal redemption.
